Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Arco is a habit app built on cycles. This page explains what data it stores, why, and what you can do with it.

What we collect

  • Account: email, display name and password (stored only as a BCrypt hash, never in plain text).
  • Google sign-in: if you use it, we receive from Google only your email, name and an account identifier. We do not access contacts, files or any other data from your Google account.
  • Your records in the app: habits, levels, check-ins, cycles, goals, focus sessions, reviews and XP.
  • Notifications: if you enable reminders, your browser's push subscription address.
  • Technical: a session cookie (needed to keep you signed in) and server access logs, used for security and to fix errors.

How we use it

  • To make the app work: show your data, calculate progress and send the reminders you enabled.
  • To send account emails: email confirmation and password recovery. We do not send advertising.

We do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising. There are no third-party trackers or ads.

Who processes the data

  • Microsoft Azure: hosting of the app and database.
  • Resend: delivery of account emails.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Browser push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla): delivery of notifications, when enabled.

Your data is yours alone

Each account is isolated: no other user sees your records. At any time, in the app's Profile, you can:

  • Export all your data as JSON;
  • Delete your account, which permanently erases the account and all records tied to it.

Demo accounts and their data are automatically deleted 24 hours after creation. Server backups are kept for up to 14 days and then discarded.

Security

Access is over HTTPS only; passwords and API keys are stored as hashes; the session cookie is HttpOnly and Secure.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
