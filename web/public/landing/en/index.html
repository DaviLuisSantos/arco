FAQ

The essentials, straight to the point.

What happens if I miss a day?

Mark “Minimum” to keep the habit alive or “Missed” without guilt. Arco shows your consistency over the period and does not punish a single day.

Who decides when to level up?

You do. The app suggests it when the habit's rule is met (for example, 80% over 14 days), but the decision is always manual.

Do I need to be online?

Not for check-ins. They are stored and sent when the connection returns. Other actions need a network.

Can I use it outside the app?

Yes. Every feature is available through the API with your own key, and there is an MCP server for use with AI assistants.
